ChillScreen 1.1.0

A more personal Automatic daily cycle, responsive room-light adaptation, and deeper control without giving up a direct Manual mode.

macOS 14 Sonoma or laterReleased 31 August 2026
Download DMGSHA-256 checksum

Ambient Light adaptation

  • Optional Ambient Light adaptation can add extra dimming and warmth when the room becomes darker.
  • ChillScreen lists the light sensors it can read, lets you exclude individual sensors, and uses the brightest enabled reading.
  • A dedicated calibration tool controls when adaptation begins, when it reaches full strength, and the maximum extra Dimming and Warmth.
  • Calibration includes a live maximum preview and conservative reset defaults.
  • Ambient-light readings and calibration stay on this Mac.

Learning that remains under your control

  • Optional Learning personalizes future Automatic evenings from your adjustments.
  • Your choice stays exact for the current evening; Learning influences the next evening instead of fighting the control you are moving.
  • Learning History shows saved adjustments and recommendation logic, with Undo, adjustable model weights, and a history reset that does not erase your Manual schedule.
  • Learning data stays on this Mac and never requires an account.

Deeper display calibration

  • Advanced Settings lets you define what 100% Warmth and 100% Dimming mean on your displays.
  • Green and blue output can be tuned independently for a deeper red maximum.
  • Maximum-effect and Ambient Light calibration both provide live previews and safe reset defaults.

Daily Cycle, reliability, and performance

  • Automatic and Manual keep separate Wake, Evening, and Bedtime positions and restore the correct set when modes change.
  • Local sunrise and sunset are calculated on the Mac from optional Location access; no coordinate entry or network service is required.
  • Transitions, previews, mode changes, relaunch, and display changes use a smooth two-second catch-up, including the return from a preview.
  • Ambient sensing recovers after sleep and display changes, and a missing new reading does not invent a change in the room.
  • Stable display-table maintenance was reduced to 10 Hz, substantially lowering steady-state CPU use while preserving smooth transitions.
  • Diagnostics is available inside Advanced Settings instead of occupying the everyday menu.

Availability and pricing

Every feature remains available. Nothing expires or becomes locked.

Personal use, nonprofit organizations, and government educational organizations may use ChillScreen for free. After two weeks, ChillScreen asks what the app is worth to you. Any amount is valid, including €0, and every choice keeps the complete app.

Commercial organizations that deploy or manage ChillScreen for employees need a corporate license. See the repository Terms of Use.

Privacy

ChillScreen does not capture or analyze screen contents. Schedules, Learning, Ambient Light readings, calibration, and optional location coordinates remain local. There is no account, in-app analytics, advertising, or cloud synchronization.

Before installing

This release is not signed or notarized through Apple's paid developer program. On first launch, Control-click or right-click ChillScreen in Applications, choose Open, then confirm Open. The User Guide includes the alternative Open Anyway steps if macOS still blocks it.
