# ChillScreen 1.1.0

ChillScreen 1.1 makes the automatic daily cycle more personal, more responsive
to the room around you, and easier to tune without giving up direct Manual
control.

## What is new

### Ambient Light adaptation

- Optional Ambient Light adaptation can add extra dimming and warmth when the
  room becomes darker.
- ChillScreen lists the light sensors it can read, lets you exclude individual
  sensors, and uses the brightest enabled reading.
- A dedicated calibration tool lets you choose when adaptation begins, when it
  reaches full strength, and the maximum extra dimming and warmth.
- Calibration includes a live maximum preview and conservative reset defaults.
- Ambient-light readings and calibration stay on this Mac.

### Learning that remains under your control

- Optional Learning personalizes future Automatic evenings from the
  adjustments you make.
- Your choice stays exact for the current evening; Learning influences the next
  evening rather than fighting the control you are moving now.
- Learning History shows the saved adjustments and recommendation logic, with
  Undo, adjustable model weights, and a history reset that does not erase your
  Manual schedule.
- Learning data stays on this Mac and never requires an account.

### Deeper display calibration

- Advanced Settings now lets you define what 100% Warmth and 100% Dimming mean
  on your own displays.
- Green and blue output can be tuned independently for a deeper red maximum.
- Both maximum-effect and Ambient Light calibration provide live previews and
  safe reset defaults.

### Daily Cycle and reliability improvements

- Automatic and Manual keep separate Wake, Evening, and Bedtime positions and
  restore the correct set when you change modes.
- Local sunrise and sunset are calculated on the Mac from optional Location
  access; no coordinate entry or network service is required.
- Transitions, previews, mode changes, relaunch, and display changes use a
  smooth two-second catch-up, including the return from a preview.
- Ambient sensing recovers after sleep and display changes, and a missing new
  reading does not invent a change in the room.
- Stable display-table maintenance was reduced to 10 Hz, substantially lowering
  steady-state CPU use while preserving smooth 30 fps transitions.
- Diagnostics is available inside Advanced Settings instead of occupying the
  everyday menu.

## Availability and pricing

Every ChillScreen feature remains available. Nothing expires or becomes
locked.

Personal use, nonprofit organizations, and government educational
organizations may use ChillScreen for free. After two weeks, ChillScreen asks
what the app is worth to you. Any amount is valid, including **€0**, and every
choice keeps the complete app.

Commercial organizations that deploy or manage ChillScreen for employees need
a corporate license. See the repository
[Terms of Use](https://github.com/EJeer/ChillScreen/blob/main/docs/terms-of-use.md).

## Privacy

ChillScreen does not capture or analyze screen contents. Schedules, Learning,
Ambient Light readings, calibration, and optional location coordinates remain
local. There is no account, analytics, advertising, or cloud synchronization.

## Before installing

This release is not signed or notarized through Apple's paid developer
program. On first launch, Control-click or right-click ChillScreen in
Applications, choose **Open**, then confirm **Open**. See the repository README
for the alternative **Open Anyway** steps if macOS still blocks it.

## Requirements

- macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
