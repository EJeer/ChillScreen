# Why I built ChillScreen

I built ChillScreen because I couldn't find a screen tool that worked the way
I wanted.

I've had some trouble with sleep for a long time, and one thing I wanted to
control better was my exposure to bright and blue light in the evening.

macOS Night Shift never really worked for me. The transition felt too limited,
the screen never became red enough, and in practice it felt more like an on/off
switch than something I could truly tune around my evening.

I tried other apps too. Most of them either solved only part of the problem or
put the features I actually wanted behind a roughly $15 paywall.

So I decided to make my own. :)

ChillScreen gradually adjusts two things independently: screen dimming and
red-light intensity. Both curves can be customised instead of following a fixed
preset.

It can also use the Mac's ambient light sensor, so when the room gets darker,
ChillScreen can automatically increase dimming and red light based on the actual
lux level.

There's a learning mode too. Instead of assuming that one brightness or colour
curve works for every condition and everyone, ChillScreen can learn from the
adjustments you make and adapt to your preferences in the future.

I also wanted to visually understand *when* and how these changes happen, not
just set a timer at some point of the day.

So ChillScreen has a daily cycle where you set your wake-up time, bedtime,
evening transition, and the point when you want the maximum effect. It
visualises the whole day together with reference circadian rhythms, so you can
actually see how your screen settings relate to your sleep/wake schedule.

And pretty much everything can be adjusted manually if you want to go deeper.

At first, I was making this purely for myself.

But somewhere along the way it started becoming the tool I had been looking for
in the first place. So I decided it was worth sharing.

ChillScreen remains free for personal use, nonprofit organizations, and public
education. After two weeks, you can decide what it is worth to you — and €0 is
always a complete choice. No features are locked.
