Science behind ChillScreen

ChillScreen is informed by published research on human circadian rhythms, melatonin timing, the cortisol awakening response, and the effects of evening light exposure on melatonin.

This research guides the biological rhythms shown in ChillScreen and how warmth and dimming are applied throughout the day.
