# ChillScreen privacy

ChillScreen is designed to work locally on the Mac.

- Screen contents are not captured, recorded, transmitted, or analyzed.
- A one-time location fix is optional. It is used to calculate local sunrise
  and sunset, and the resulting coordinates stay in the app's local preferences.
- Learning history and its model settings remain local.
- Ambient-light readings and calibration remain local.
- ChillScreen has no account, analytics, advertising, or cloud synchronization.
- The optional value prompt stores its start date and completion state locally
  on the Mac.
- Choosing €0 does not contact any server. Opening **Buy me a coffee**, a
  corporate email link, or a GitHub link leaves the app and opens the external
  service. ChillScreen does not receive payment status or email contents.

Removing ChillScreen does not automatically remove its local macOS preferences.
